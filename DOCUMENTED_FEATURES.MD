- Add to Shopping list
- Remove from shopping list
- Clear all items from the shopping list
- Add recipe items to shopping list
- Add a new recipe (name, description, time, serves, and ingredients entered one per line as "name, amount")
- Search recipes by name
- Adjust a recipe's servings with +/- buttons; ingredient amounts scale to match, and the scaled amounts are added to the shopping list
- New recipe flow (4 steps with a progress bar, Back/Next, and Cancel):
  1. Add recipe: enter name, description, time, serves, and ingredients one per line as "name, amount" (name is required)
  2. Shopping list: tick which ingredients to add to the shopping list, or skip
  3. Rate: give the recipe 1-5 stars and an optional note, or skip; the rating shows on the recipe card and details
  4. Share: preview the recipe as text and copy it to the clipboard; Done returns to the recipe book with the new recipe selected
